Safety: firmware 2.0.1 has no hardware e-stop. Keep the power switch in reach. Before any trace/plot, motors off, carriage at the chosen anchor point (top-left, top-centre or centre), pen up over paper. A preflight dry run runs automatically and must be clean. E-STOP (or Ctrl-C in the terminal) raises the pen and releases the motors; an in-flight move still finishes — cut power for an instant stop.

Raster conversion

One-line: one continuous line that packs tightly inside the dark shapes. Density sets the number of passes; Dark-shape cutoff decides which shapes count as dark (a black/white mask of them appears next to the preview).
Halftone: Density sets dot fineness. Contours: a higher cutoff traces more nested outlines. Tone levels posterises the photo into 2–6 discrete values before routing, so each value becomes a nested band of ink — useful for photos. Dark-shape cutoff still sets the mask when continuous. Lower cutoff = darker areas become ink. With more than one ink layer, Plot stops for a manual pen swap. Drawings are automatically fitted inside the reachable canvas (1.5 cm clear of every paper edge). All of these are ignored for SVG uploads.

Paper & placement

Only the dashed canvas is reachable — it stays 15 mm clear of every paper edge, and uploads are auto-fitted to it. Slide the carriage by hand to the orange dot — the chosen anchor — pen up, before every run. Top-centre is usually easiest to eyeball: place the carriage at the middle of the sheet's top edge and the drawing will be centred on it, inset 15 mm from the edges automatically. A dry run refuses to start if the carriage is off the paper or the drawing is off the canvas. Defaults come from config/machine.json.
