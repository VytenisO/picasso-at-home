                          Setting up the plotter
                          
    
                          
    
                          
    
                          
    
                          
    
                          
      
      
      
      
      
    
                          
  
                          
                          


  
                          
    
                          Help
                          
      
      
                          
    
                          
      
      
      
    
                          
    
                          
      
                          
        
                            
          
                          1. Prepare an image, PDF, 3D model or text: build a recipe, check the result in ink, Send to Plot. A finished SVG skips this: Import it in Plot.
                            2. 
          
                          2. In Plot, add drawings from the Library and arrange them on the bed. Everything stays inside the dashed drawable area.
                            3. 
          
                          3. Pick a pen per layer under Layers; speeds and path order under Job.
                            4. 
          
                          4. Preview checks the plan. Simulate runs it with nothing moving.
                            5. 
          
                          5. Motors off, slide the carriage by hand to S (the paper's top-left), pen up.
                            6. 
          
                          6. Plot. Between layers it pauses for a pen change.
                            7. 
        
                          
        
                          Stopping
                          
        
                          Stop lifts the pen and switches the motors off. On firmware 3.x the move in flight is cut short; older
          firmware finishes it first (up to 3 s). Return finishes the stroke and travels back to S. For an
          instant stop, switch the plotter off.
                          
        
                          The position shown is the commanded one, counted from S. If the carriage was bumped or the motors
          were off, it is wrong until you park it again.

          and the log, for a bug report.
                          
      
                          
    
                          
  
                          
                          


  
                          
    
                          Text on the bed
                          
    
                          Single-line letters: each stroke of a letter is drawn once. Placed where you clicked.
                          
    
    
                          
      
      
      
      
    
                          
    
                          
      
      
    
                          
    
                          
      Several from a list (name tags, numbered cards)

        comma-separated columns.
                          
      
      
    
                          
    
                          
  
                          
                          


  
                          
    
                          Shape
                          
    
    
    
                          
      
      
    
                          
    
                          
      
      
    
                          
    
    
    
    
                          Then click on the bed where its top-left corner goes.
                          
    
                          Then drag on the bed to draw one line; it is smoothed a little (0.3 mm).
                          
    
                          Then on the bed: click for a corner, press and drag for a smooth point (the drag sets its
      handles). Click the first point to close; Enter finishes, Backspace removes the last point, Esc cancels.
                          
    
                          Then click on the bed for each point. Shift keeps 45 degree steps. Click the first point to
      close the shape; double-click or Enter finishes an open line; Esc cancels; Backspace removes the last point.
