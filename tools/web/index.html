      Trash

        Projects

        A project is the whole bed: the drawings, where they sit, pens and speeds.

          Layers and pens

          Each ink layer is plotted in turn; the plot pauses for a pen change between layers. Settings left empty use the job defaults. Pressure is a fraction from pen-down (0, heaviest) toward pen-up, at most 0.9.

          Open a drawing to see its layers.

          Filled shapes

          Shapes with a fill colour, by colour. A pen cannot fill, so a fill becomes lines: hatching (parallel lines, optionally crossed) or contour rings that follow the outline inward. The fill lines get a layer of their own colour above, with its own pen.

          Pen tests

          Test sheets and their results belong to one pen type. A result for a ballpoint says nothing about a marker.

          Live writing

          Type a line, press Write, and the plotter writes it as you go. Needs the carriage parked at S, like a plot. Use a font, or your own handwriting once you have captured samples.

          Sport mode is above the tested speed (40 mm/s). The motors can lose steps and the position is not trusted afterwards. Use it rarely.

          SPORT MODE: the pad drives the plotter at up to 100 mm/s. Re-park the carriage afterwards.

            Control

            Position unknown

            Move the carriage from here: jog, go to a point, pen up and down, or set S exactly on the paper corner. Starts like a plot, with the carriage parked by hand at S and the pen up. The position is counted from S; nothing can be read back from this firmware.

            Arrow keys on the bed jog one step too.

            Saved positions
              Register on marks already on the paper

              For a second pass on a sheet that came out: Add registration marks before the first plot. With the sheet back in, jog the pen tip onto each mark and press its button; the next preview turns and shifts the whole bed to match.

              Find the centre of a round object

              A plate, a coaster, a lid: jog the pen tip to three points on its edge, spread around it.

              Macros

                One command per line: GOTO x y, MOVE dx dy, PEN UP, PEN DOWN, HOME, TICK dx dy, SPEED v, WAIT ms. Moves stay inside the reachable area and the position stays known.

                EBB command line (advanced)

                Sends one command straight to the board. Bootloader, reset, flash, configuration and servo setup commands are refused. Anything that moves or frees the carriage makes its position unknown.

                        How plotting works here
                        
    
                        Loading versions...
                        
    
                        Download a diagnostic bundle (versions, machine and pen settings, the last jobs and
      log) to attach to a bug report.
                        
    
                          
      
                        1. Prepare (optional): drop an image, 3D model or SVG, or start from text. Build a recipe (adjust,
        method, clean up), check the result in ink, choose pens, and send it to Plot. A finished SVG can skip this.
                          2. 
      
                        2. In Plot, add drawings from the Library. Several can share the bed.
                          3. 
      
                        3. Arrange them: drag to move, drag a handle to resize (sides stretch, corners keep proportions), drag the round
        handle to turn. Or type the numbers under Arrange. Everything must stay inside the dashed canvas.
                          4. 
      
                        4. Set pens and speeds per layer in Layers.
                          5. 
      
                        5. Preview checks the plan with the plotter's own rules. Simulate runs it on a virtual machine.
                          6. 
      
                        6. Switch the plotter on with the motors off, slide the carriage by hand to S (the paper's top-left), pen up.
                          7. 
      
                        7. Start plot, confirm, and watch. Between layers it pauses for a pen change.
                          8. 
    
                        
    
                        Shortcuts
                        
    
                        
    
                        Stopping
                        
    
                        This firmware (EBB 2.0.1) has no emergency stop and no position readback. Stop finishes the move already
      sent (normally within 3 s), lifts the pen and releases the motors; the carriage stays where it stopped.
      Return to start finishes the current stroke and travels back to S. For an instant stop, switch the plotter off.
                        
    
                        The carriage position shown is the commanded one, counted from S. If the carriage was bumped or
      the motors were off, it is wrong until you park it again.
